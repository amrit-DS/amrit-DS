### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 🏠 [Queensland Rental Affordability Analysis](https://github.com/amrit-DS/queensland-rental-affordability)

End-to-end Python analysis combining RTA rental bond data with ABS Census household income to measure how 2-bedroom flat affordability shifted across 143 Queensland postcodes between the 2016 and 2021 Censuses. Cleaned and joined three messy government datasets, applied a data-reliability filter to exclude low-sample postcodes, and validated geographic labels against known values after catching a broken source column. Found affordability stayed broadly stable at the median as incomes kept pace with rents, but shifted geographically, worsening across coastal and regional centres while improving across Brisbane. Published with full documentation and an **[interactive map](https://amrit-ds.github.io/queensland-rental-affordability/qld_affordability_map.html)**.

`Python` · `pandas` · `Data Cleaning` · `Data Validation` · `Geospatial` · `folium`

#### 📚 [NLP Textbook Recommendation Engine](https://github.com/amrit-DS/nlp-textbook-recommender)

Combines title similarity with subject metadata into a hybrid score, and evaluates with honest intrinsic metrics (catalogue coverage, list diversity, subject consistency) rather than fabricated supervised scores.

`NLP` · `TF-IDF` · `scikit-learn` · `Recommender Systems` · `Python`
